## Presentation reflections

Hard to believe that the semester is already over and that everyone has for the most part wrapped up their contributions for their respective projects. <!--more-->I am pretty happy with what my group was able to accomplish in our short time and think that our presentation went well. I am usually bad with presentations and have to do a lot of practice for them, but I found that it was not that hard to talk about what we worked on for Keycloak. It was comforting to hear that it was a common experience for other students to also have a hard time finding good issues to work on. It was very insightful, I think I will look for alternative things to contribute to in the future. 

## Personal contributions

I was able to make a bug fix on an open source rhythm game called Osu. Previously, when you entered the game you were able to press f3 to open the options menu but if you pressed it again, it would not close. Upon first glance this sounded like a pretty easy issue to fix, but the way that the logic was being handled meant that I would also have to do work on their separate framework repository as well. Since there was no way to pass the state of the menu to the main repository, I had to add the logic myself. Here is the [pr for the main repo](https://github.com/ppy/osu/pull/37648) and the [pr for the framework repo](https://github.com/ppy/osu-framework/pull/6743). After a lot of scouring through both repositories I believe I was able to come up with a pretty efficient solution, although I can only hope that the maintainers think so too. I hope that the pull requests will get merged and now that I am more familiar with this codebase I may also look into more issues that I can work on. They even have a system for requesting compensation for your work if you have made frequent or sizable contributions. I am pretty proud of the changes I made, this is definitely something I would not have been able to do at the beginning of the semester and it makes me excited to engage in more open source projects in the future.
