## Group progress

Our group has been steadily making progress over the last two weeks. <!--more-->Over break some group members opened a pull request and fixed some documentations issues. Ben and Albert continue to work on the translation support issue and seem to be making good progress on that. Sebastian and I have begun work on an issue regarding[session timeout per organization.](https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak/issues/46549) Previously session timeouts were only managed on a larger realm basis or a smaller per user basis. Our hope is to implement a feature which allows people to control this on a per organization basis. To be honest, I am still not completely familiar with the codebase but I think this will be a great opportunity to delve deeper and make a more meaningful contribution.

## Personal contributions

My personal contributions had been very lacking up till this point in the semester so I decided to get on OpenStreetMap and find anything I can possibly contribute. When I had looked at it in the past, I saw that all of the parks I knew were clearly labeled so I was unsure what to edit. However, now that I have revisited it I realize that the contributions I can make lie in opened and closed restaurants and shops within my area. This realization led me to my first contribution on OpenStreetMap and I plan on making another in the near future. On top of that, I plan on finding an issue on a different open source project that I could maybe do on my own. Since I think it would be difficult to figure out another codebase on top of the Keycloak one our group is working on, I imagine I will look for a more easier documentation fix. I hope this will give me the momentum I need to get over this impostor syndrome I have when it comes to contributing to open source.
