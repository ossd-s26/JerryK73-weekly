## Group work

Since our group is presenting next week, we have been mainly focusing on making sure we are ready for our presentation.<!--more--> Sebastian and I were finally able to create that [PR](https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak/pull/48376) for our organization session timeout feature. I did recently see though that the maintainer replied to it saying that we will have to wait for the core team to review it and that our solution seemed complex. A little bit of a bummer since we tried communicating in the early stages of our development, but I am still proud of the work and effort that we put into this. This is going to be my last post regarding Keycloak since we will have presented by next week. Reflecting on my experience, I think I have learned a lot about how open source projects work in general and how I can contribute to them. Before this semester I definitely did not see open source projects that I, as an inexperienced programmer, could provide any value to but my perspective on that has shifted a bit. Maybe it won't be on Keycloak but I definitely see myself contributing to other open source projects I run into in the future.
