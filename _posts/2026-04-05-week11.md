## Thoughts on The Cathedral and the Bazaar

I feel like reading "The Cathedral and the Bazaar" really showed me how effective open source can be as opposed to closed source methods. <!--more-->I remember hearing the opinion at some point that open source is good but closed source software is often higher quality but this reading proves that this is not necessarily the case. It is kind of interesting to imagine how back in the day, the idea of an open source project was a new and unconventional method of doing things. Maybe it is because open source has been longer around than I have on this planet, but I feel like having more eyes on a project just makes more sense intuitively and I often take for granted the innovation that took place before me. 

As for AI tools, I feel like they bring both pros and cons to the table. They definitely make it more accessible for new contributors to try and make contributions of their own. However, if these new contributors are not careful, they could submit code that they do not even understand and end up creating more work for maintainers. AI tools definitely can help in areas like debugging but I do not think that it should be used in every aspect of development.

## Group work

Group work is progressing smoothly. Sebastian and I continued working on the [session timeout issue](https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak/issues/46549) and Ben and Albert continued with the translation support. I would say Sebastian and I are about halfway through working on this issue. It has been challenging in the sense that it has forced me to become more familiar with the codebase and make changes on around 6 different files so far. The changes primarily have consisted of creating columns for the JPA database for our new organization specific timeout values, as well as making a bunch of getter and setter methods for them. I have not run into any issues or bugs yet but am kind of scared for when we finish implementing the majority of the feature and have to see if Keycloak still builds. It builds completely fine currently, but I doubt that will continue to be the case.
