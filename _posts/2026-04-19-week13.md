## Open Projects

This week we researched and discussed open source projects that are not directly within the tech space.<!--more--> The project that I found and talked about is called e-NABLE and it is a volunteer led community that focuses on creating and distributing designs for 3D printing prosthetic limbs. Since traditional medical prosthetics cost a ton of money and often have to be replaced by children that are fast to outgrow them, a 3D printed alternative is a great option. Other than that, OpenStax and Open Education Resources stood out to me as projects that make education more accessible, which is something that I find important in today's world. Although I am not equipped at all to study astronomy, I also found Stellarium to be very cool and clearly useful to people who engage in that area of study.
