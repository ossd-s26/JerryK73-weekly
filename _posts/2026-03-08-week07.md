## Working Agreement and Choosing a Project

This week we were assigned our groups and were tasked with creating a working agreement as well as deciding which project we wanted to work on. <!--more-->We had met previously as well to narrow down our options for which project to choose. We had a lot of projects come up such as ActivityWatch (an open source time tracker), Cython, Pandas, Gimp, Godot Engine, and Keycloak. After weighing the pros and cons of each project and seeing how active each community was, we ended up with just Pandas and Keycloak to choose between. Personally I had never used either of these, so I did not have much of a perference between the two and it seemed Albert felt the same. Sebastian was more in favor for Pandas and Ben preferred Keycloak, but after some deliberation Keycloak won out. Overall I think it was a good choice, seeing that the community is very active and that one of us has a lot of experience with it already. It is kind of daunting thinking about contributing to a well established open source project with many contributors already, but I am excited to see what our group accomplishes together. 

The [working agreement](https://github.com/ossd-s26/wiki/wiki/Group-F-Working-Agreement) activity went very smoothly and we did not run into any issues there. We agreed on matters when creating it and I guess it will be useful in the unlikely but possible chance that conflicts do arise between us.
