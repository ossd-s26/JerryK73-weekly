## Business models around open source

The discussion regarding how businesses engage with open source and how some even create business models around it was very illuminating.<!--more--> Learning about Red Hat stood out to me, it was incredibly cool that they were pioneers in what they did and how they were able to become a billion-dollar company while being heavily intertwined with open source. Before this, I had only known the name Red Hat very loosely as they are the ones who actually released Keycloak back in 2014. I also heard something about them having their own Linux distro, but did not look into that further. Another thing that I really found interesting in this discussion was talking about the film industry and how they engage with open source. When 3D models for common effects were first being created it made more sense to keep them to yourself as a competitive edge, but as they became more commonplace it turned more into a handicap. Having these assets be open source nowadays allows for more streamlined creative processes that allow people focus more on their creative vision rather than more minute details. Things like this kind of put into perspective how much development and innovation happens behind the scenes for even what on the surface appears to be simple issues.
